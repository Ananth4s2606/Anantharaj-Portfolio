Problem

Students often list skills based on self-assessment, while recruiters may not have enough evidence to determine whether those skills are demonstrated.

Solution

SkillBridge AI combines skill assessment, practical verification, project evidence and AI-assisted evaluation to distinguish self-declared skills from verified skills.
